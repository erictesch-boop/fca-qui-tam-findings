Ranked findings · highest impact first

Anonymous ranked list of what the screens produced. Dollar figures are program-payment scale or exposure indicators — not proven damages.

National skin-substitute graft surge

About 430 billers rose from roughly $0.7B to roughly $2.5B. Flagged billers ~$270K–$910K per patient vs ~$60K national median (~4.5–15×).

~$2.5B band

Durable Medical Equipment (DME) supplier surges (wound dressings / catheters / related)

New and multi-hundred-million paid suppliers; screen independently ranked a later-charged supplier #1 before we knew that fact.

$100M–$400M+

Formulaic Durable Medical Equipment (DME) referral pattern

~311 referring practitioners across ~30 states with near-identical referred totals (~$0.9–1.15M, ~$8K/beneficiary) — about $304M combined.

~$304M pattern

State graft cluster (nine billers)

One-state group rose from roughly $30M to roughly $220M in a year; seven of nine had no prior graft payments; individual growth up to ~40×.

~$220M YoY

Clinical lab / genetic panel outliers

Florida and national labs with multi-million to tens-of-millions paid, extreme $/beneficiary, new National Provider Identifiers (NPIs), and panel-unbundling code patterns.

$10M–$60M class

LA County hospice & home-health clusters

Hundreds of high-score agencies/hospices (new since ~2021, ≥$1M paid) concentrated in LA / San Fernando Valley corridors.

Cluster-scale

Product area vs application codes

For several graft billers, billed product square centimeters exceeded max application-code area by about 2–3.8×.

2–3.8× mismatch

Code-window & over-frequency grafting

Volume forced into short Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) effective windows; ~12–13 applications per patient in under 26 weeks vs contractor episode norms.

Timing flag

Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) / COVID-relief ratio & twin-loan screens

Jobs-cap ratio outliers, identical twin entities, address clusters, charged-off loans — with statute-timing caveats documented.

$0.25M–$4M+ loans

Open Payments × product manufacturer gaps

Debt-forgiveness records in Open Payments; makers of products some leads billed do not appear for those leads — points to distributor/rebate records.

Cross-check

Ambulance repetitive non-emergency profiles

National/NJ/IL/GA patterns: very high trips per beneficiary on non-emergency codes; FL growth leads more modest.

$1M–$7M class

Patient-population & place-of-service mismatch

Top-decile dementia share with top-decile $/patient; all non-facility grafting; few eval visits vs hundreds of applications.

Screen flag

Flow-of-funds timing (Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) / property)

Public lien terminations and real-property transactions roughly $0.6M–$3.3M close in time to billing surges — timing context only.

$0.6M–$3.3M

Enforcement-clearance validation

Method ranked a later-publicly-charged supplier first without being told — evidence the screen works; cleared leads kept; charged/settled set aside.
